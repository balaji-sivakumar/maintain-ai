In-Ground Pool Pump — Maintenance Guide (excerpt)

Routine care: Clean the pump strainer basket every 2 weeks during swim
season. Inspect the shaft seal for leaks and lubricate O-rings once every
6 months to prevent premature seal failure.

Typical lifespan: Pool pump motors are generally rated for about 8–12
years (use 10 years as a planning estimate) before bearing wear and motor
inefficiency make replacement more economical than continued repair.

Repair costs: Seal or motor bearing repairs typically run $100–$350.
Full motor replacement (keeping the existing pump housing) runs
$250–$600.

Replacement costs: A full pump replacement, including a modern
variable-speed motor for efficiency, typically costs $700–$1,800
installed.
