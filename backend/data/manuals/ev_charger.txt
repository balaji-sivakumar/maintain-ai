Level 2 Home EV Charger — Owner's Manual (excerpt)

Maintenance: Inspect the charging cable, connector, and mounting bracket for
wear or damage once a year. A qualified electrician should re-torque the
electrical panel connections annually as part of routine home electrical
maintenance. No internal parts are user-serviceable.

Expected service life: Most home EV chargers are designed for approximately
10 years of regular use before internal components (relays, connectors)
degrade enough to warrant replacement.

Repair costs: Common repairs include cable replacement ($150–$400) or
connector/relay replacement by a licensed electrician ($200–$600), depending
on labor rates in your area.

Replacement costs: A new Level 2 home charging unit plus professional
installation typically runs $500–$2,200, depending on amperage, brand, and
whether existing wiring/panel capacity is sufficient.
