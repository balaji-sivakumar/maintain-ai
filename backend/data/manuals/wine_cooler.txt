Dual-Zone Wine Cooler — Care & Maintenance Guide (excerpt)

Routine care: Clean the condenser coils every 6 months to keep the
compressor running efficiently. Check the door seal annually and wipe
down interior shelving.

Typical lifespan: A dual-zone wine cooler compressor unit is typically
rated for around 8 years of continuous operation before compressor
failure becomes likely.

Repair costs: Compressor or thermostat repairs generally run $120–$450
depending on the part and whether refrigerant needs recharging.

Replacement costs: A comparable replacement dual-zone unit typically
costs $350–$1,200 depending on capacity and brand.
